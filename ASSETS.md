# DIVE asset provenance

Athlete.fbx, SwimHands.fbx and athlete-rigged.blend: original models created for this project with Blender. 15 deform bones, weighted skin, Idle / Swim / Shoot animation clips. Build source: scripts/create-rigged-diver.py. These original models remain as source references and first-person hands. The world players now use the Meshy model described below.

Pool architecture, bubbles, floor caustics and underwater surface shaders: original project assets. Water uses a 512px planar reflection, screen-space refraction and animated wave normals; it is a real-time visual approximation, not a fluid simulation.

Poly Haven licensing was researched as an option; no Poly Haven assets were downloaded or included in this revision.

## Audio

Ambience, Swim, Shot and Impact were generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2. Music is a 20-second instrumental generated with ElevenLabs Music. Six announcer clips use ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 and the stock voice JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb. Original requests and provider-reported credit costs are recorded in art/audio/elevenlabs/*.json. Generated audio is governed by the user's ElevenLabs plan and terms; it is not claimed as CC0.

Pass, Pickup, Start, Goal, Concede, Win and Lose are original synthesized cues generated by scripts/create-audio.mjs. The ElevenLabs scripts replace only their named clips. Raw generated MP3s are retained under art/audio/elevenlabs; normalized 24 kHz WAVs ship with Unity. Keys are read only by local generation scripts and are excluded from Git and the Web build.


Poolside garden, clubhouse, benches, paving and palm meshes are original procedural Unity assets in DivePoolside.cs. The splash reposes the original diver rig and uses a dedicated camera and portrait light. No extra asset purchases were made for this environment.


## Meshy human diver

One human model generated with Meshy 7, textured, auto-rigged with 24 bones, then retextured to a closed full wetsuit while preserving UVs. The four jobs used 45 credits total (20 model, 10 texture, 5 rig, 10 correction). No second character or paid environment generation was needed. The user account plan and Meshy terms govern these generated assets; no CC0 claim is made.

`art/meshy/diver-production.blend` retains the skeleton, corrected suit and preview studio. `MeshyDiver.fbx`, `DiverColor.png` and `DiverNormal.png` are the Unity resources. `DiveHuman` provides procedural skeletal motion; `DiveTeamAppearance` and the AthleteTeam shader color the suits while retaining skin. Meshy's original material included an old emissive texture; that glow connection was removed in Blender and is not used by the Unity team shader.

Poolside materials use original procedural stone and timber texture variation. No external hockey-game assets were imported. Made with AnkleBreaker MCP; retain its required attribution when distributing the project.
