# Game assets

Game-ready models, textures, audio, and shaders live in `unity/Assets/Dive/`. Intermediate exports and generation requests stay local and are not required to rebuild the game.

The blue and coral teams share a rigged human character. First-person hands are separate assets. Pool geometry and environment materials are procedural. Water uses planar reflections, refraction, waves, and caustics as a visual approximation.

See [Credits](docs/credits/README.md) for required third-party attribution and asset terms.
